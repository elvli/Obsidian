John 9
We have been held captive by ourselves thinking we are our own God.

The self as become magnified, leaving less room for God. It tried to make itself look better, comparing itself to other. 

Jesus calls us to put our “selfs” to death, then you will experience the things of God that comes into our lives

Jesus is helping the blind see Jesus for who he really is. The blind man saw the beauty in Christ, John 9:6-7

1. See Jesus
	1. Th Pharisees were stuck in their own lives, they could not see the truth of Jesus and his miracle. They were so stuck with their knowledge and traditions. 
	2. Pharisees were born with sight yet spiritually blind. 
2. Remove the Blinds
	1. He surrenders himself, falling before Jesus
	2. All we’ve been doing is pointing at other people
	3. Point at yourself, then you can follow the first toe commandments
3. Worship Jesus
