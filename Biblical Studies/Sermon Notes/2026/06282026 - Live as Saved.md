John 5:1-17

The invalid man was completely hopeless and his testimony was full of despair. Sometime we magnify our own problem and make that everything. So much so that he couldn’t see the blessings of your lives. 

Rest in the things of this world, don’t rest in the word.

Jesus wants us to:
1. Live in Hope
2. Live in Obedience
3. Live in Freedom

Don’t waste the talents that God has given you
