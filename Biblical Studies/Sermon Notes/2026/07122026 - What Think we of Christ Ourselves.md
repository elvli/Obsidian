John 6:66-71; 7:1-24

What think we of Christ affects:
1. Our will to **Embrace** him fully
	1. We’ve all at one point or another turned to the gods of the world. Wanting money, prosperity, or other things
	2. We all have to turn from what is good feeling, seek God first and everything will be given to you
	3. We can be ignorant of the Person of Christ. Not even his brothers believe in him
2. Our will to be **Empowered** to receive and be obedient to him
	1. John 7:11-14
	2. A church chasing novelty does not have a firm foundation
	3. To be used by God, you don’t have to be perfect, you have to be available and obedient 
3. Our will to be **Endowed** with enlightenment of his word

Does man have free will? 
Does man choose God?
Does God choose man?
Or a combination of both?

God’s will, how would I know?
John 7:17, if anyone’s will is to do God’s will, he will know whether the teaching is from God or whether I am speaking on my own authority.

We have become spiritually lazy in the we only want to experience the enjoyments of Jesus and feeling in his presence, but we do not strive to take the initiative to seek God first, take the time to pray, and read his word.
