John 13 
- This is the last supper, Jesus knows that Judas will betray him

Are you being wash by the word?

1. Choose the towel over the throne
	1. John 3:3 unless one is born again, one can not see the kingdom of God
	2. We have to be washed again and again even after baptism
2. Guard the heart, against small compromises
	1. Judas was unclean from the beginning, this is what allowed Satan to enter his heart
	2. Temptation will come when we least expect it to come
3. Let every relationship be marked with Christ like love
	1. If let God clean your feet, he will clean your heart
